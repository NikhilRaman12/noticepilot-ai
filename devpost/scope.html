NoticePilot AI

Turn one confusing public-service notice into next steps a citizen can verify against the source.

Draft · for learner review

The distinctive idea

Every important result stays inspectable: people can see the notice passage behind a deadline, requirement, or action, while ambiguous, missing, or conflicting information is surfaced rather than guessed or silently resolved.

One user, one verifiable loop

Who it's for

A nontechnical citizen whose public-service notice requests additional documents before a deadline, and who needs to know what to do without repeatedly rereading it.

What makes it matter

It applies practical AI to make complex information easier to understand and act on, while practicing a disciplined plan-first workflow.

Feel and tone

Simple, polished, approachable, clear, and calm. Prioritize inspectable evidence over confident-sounding answers.

What “working” looks like

In a short on-screen demo, a first-time user goes from a bundled fictional notice to its explanation, deadline, required actions, and verifiable checklist in under a minute. Selecting an important result reveals its exact supporting passage and location where available. One ambiguous instruction and one missing detail are flagged, not guessed. If it fits after the core workflow is reliable, conflicting dates are shown together with a warning.

The “oh, that’s cool” moment: the user discovers an answer is evidence-linked—or that the notice does not support one.

Now · the proof of concept
  • One complete, text-first workflow using a clearly fictional public-service notice; pasted text is included only if it does not delay the bundled-sample path.
  • Plain-language purpose and explanation, supported deadline and document requirements, actionable next steps, and source-linked checklist items.
  • Exact excerpts, page or section references where available, and supported, ambiguous, and not-found states. Suggestions are distinguished from notice requirements.
  • Explicit missing-information warnings; a deliberate conflicting-date example if it can be handled reliably within the remaining time.
  • A polished interface for nontechnical users, useful error handling, and transparent limitations. The main demo requires no external AI API key.
Later · after the core workflow
  • PDF upload and advanced document processing.
  • Broader notice types, real-world jurisdiction coverage, and issuing-authority integrations.
  • Capabilities not needed to prove the notice-to-verifiable-actions workflow.
Cut · not part of this proof of concept
  • Production-ready legal, government, or eligibility advice; this proof of concept is not authoritative.
  • Inventing requirements, resolving ambiguity or conflicts without evidence, or presenting unsupported interpretations as verified fact.
  • A general-purpose document platform or broad agent workflow that would make the core demo less reliable within the hackathon.
